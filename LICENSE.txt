AI DISCLOSURE
The sound effects and the twinkle-noise / starfield textures in this product
were procedurally generated with an AI-assisted toolchain on 2026-08-24,
random seed 20260823. The original generator scripts were not preserved.
Scripts/generar_audio_fuente.py and Scripts/generar_texturas_fuente.py are
reconstructions written after the fact: they document the synthesis method,
demonstrate that no third-party recordings, samples or imagery are needed
to produce these assets, and regenerate an equivalent original set from a
documented seed - comparable in character and format to the shipped files,
not byte-identical copies of them. Both scripts ship with the plugin.
This is also disclosed in the Fab listing.
